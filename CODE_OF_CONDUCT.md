# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully and keep discussion
focused on the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks and publishing another person's private information without consent.

Report conduct concerns privately to @siiick-brain through the repository's
GitHub contact options. Reports will be reviewed and handled as appropriate to
protect participants and maintain a usable project space.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other
project-managed spaces.
